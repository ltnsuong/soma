Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

SOMA works by listening to you. That only makes sense if you know exactly what is kept, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. This page says so plainly.

The short version.
  • What you tell Soma is stored on our servers so it is there tomorrow. It is not sold, rented, or used to train anyone's models.
  • Your diary, memories and the people in your Circle are never shown to other users.
  • Therapist reports are shared only when you choose to share them, each time.
  • You can delete your account, and everything attached to it, from inside the app.

Who we are

SOMA is operated by the developer of the SOMA application, reachable at lethinhutsuong@gmail.com. For privacy questions, write to that address with "Privacy" in the subject.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your email address and name. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Telegram we receive an account identifier from them, and an email address where you allow it. Passwords, where you set one, are stored only as a cryptographic hash and cannot be read back by us.
  • What you say to Soma — your conversations, diary entries, mood entries, and the notes Soma derives from them.
  • Your Circle — the names and context of people you mention. These are your private notes about your own relationships. We do not contact those people.
  • Profile information — photographs you upload, and the connection profiles Soma derives from what you have said.
  • Voice — when you speak to Soma, the audio is sent for transcription and discarded once transcribed. We do not keep recordings.

Information collected automatically

  • Approximate location, if you allow it, so matching can favour people near enough to actually meet. Your precise coordinates are never shown to another user.
  • Notification tokens, if you allow notifications.
  • Basic technical logs — request times and error records, used to keep the service running.

We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Why we hold it

  • To be your companion. Soma cannot remember you between conversations unless what you said is stored.
  • To make introductions. Your connection profiles are compared with other users' profiles to suggest people you may want to meet.
  • To run the service — signing you in, sending the emails you ask for, processing subscriptions, keeping things working.
  • To keep people safe — for example, showing crisis resources when they may be needed.

Where the GDPR applies, our lawful basis is the performance of our contract with you (running the app you signed up for), your consent (location, notifications, sharing a report with a therapist), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

What other people can see

This distinction is the heart of the product, so it is worth being exact.

Never shared with other usersShared when you choose
Your diary and mood history Your public connection profile
Your memories and conversations with Soma Photographs you add to that profile
The people in your Circle Messages you send to a match
Your exact location A therapist report, each time you send one

When two people are matched, their AI agents compare profiles privately and produce a summary. The summary is derived from your connection profile — not from your diary, your memories, or your Circle.

Where your data is held

Our database is hosted in the European Union. Some providers listed above operate in other countries, including the United States, so your information may be transferred there under the contractual safeguards those providers offer.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account it is removed from our live systems immediately, and from routine backups within 30 days. Voice recordings are discarded as soon as they are transcribed.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account → Delete all.

This permanently erases your account, profile, memories, diary, mood history, Circle, connection profiles, matches and messages. It cannot be undone. If you cannot reach the app, email lethinhutsuong@gmail.com and we will do it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, receive a copy in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Settings → Export data gives you a copy immediately. For anything else, write to us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

SOMA is not for people under 17, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

SOMA is not a medical service

Soma is a companion, not a clinician. It does not diagnose, treat, or provide medical or psychological care, and it is not a crisis service. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line — the app lists several, and findahelpline.com lists more for almost every country.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Passwords are hashed. Access to production systems is limited. No service can promise perfect security, and we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app when the change is significant.
