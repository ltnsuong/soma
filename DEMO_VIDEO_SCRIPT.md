# SOMA — investor video script

~2 minutes. About 290 words of voiceover, which is roughly 140 words a minute
with room to breathe. Read it, don't improvise — improvised founder videos run
long and bury the one moment that matters.

Two things before you record:

- **Every number here is true and checkable.** Nothing is rounded up. If you
  change a line, keep it that way — an investor who catches one inflated number
  stops believing the rest of the video.
- **Your own voice, over a screen recording.** No hired voiceover, no music
  swell. At this stage polish reads as distance from the product.

`[...]` marks something only you can fill in.

---

## 0:00 – 0:18 · The problem

**SHOW** — Nothing yet, or a slow scroll through a dating app's profile editor.

> Every app that introduces people asks you to sell yourself first.
>
> You write a bio. You pick your best photo. You perform a version of you.
>
> Then you meet, and neither of you recognises the other one.

*Pause here. Don't rush into the solution.*

---

## 0:18 – 0:32 · The insight

**SHOW** — SOMA opening. Clean, no narration on screen.

> I wanted to try the opposite. What if you never filled in a profile —
> you just talked, and the app worked out who you are from that.

---

## 0:32 – 0:55 · It listens

**SHOW** — Real onboarding. Someone typing an answer, Soma replying. Let one
full exchange play at normal speed. Do not speed it up.

> This is the first conversation. No forms, no checkboxes.
>
> It asks about yesterday, about who you talked to, about who you wish
> you'd talked to. And it remembers.
>
> Everything in your profile comes from this. You never fill anything in.

---

## 0:55 – 1:28 · The part nobody else has

**SHOW** — Open a profile in Explore, tap through to the Synergy Scan. Let the
two agents talk. **This is the video — do not cut it short.** Then the report:
score, what you each bring, the icebreaker.

> Then, when you come across someone new — your AI and their AI talk first.
>
> Privately. Before either of you decides anything.
>
> You don't get a match percentage. You get a reason. Where you actually
> overlap, what each of you brings, and how a first conversation could start.
>
> Nobody has to perform. The introduction arrives already explained.

*This section is why the video exists. Everything before it is setup.*

---

## 1:28 – 1:47 · Where it is

**SHOW** — Back out to the app. Or your face, if you're comfortable on camera —
this is the one section where being a person helps.

> It's live today at mysoma.site.
>
> Twenty-five people are using it. Twenty of them joined in the last month.
> One has had sixty things worth remembering pulled out of their conversations.
> Eight of them are messaging each other.
>
> It's pre-revenue. The iOS build is in review now.

*Say the last line plainly. Stating the stage honestly buys you more credibility
than any number in this script.*

---

## 1:47 – 2:00 · You, and the ask

**SHOW** — You, or the SOMA mark.

> [One sentence: why you are the person building this. Something only you
>  could say — not "I'm passionate about connection". What happened to you
>  that made this obvious?]
>
> I'm raising [amount] to [the specific thing it buys — 12 months of runway?
>  a second engineer? paid acquisition to test whether this spreads?].

---

## What to leave out, and why

**The mood tracker, the diary, the Wheel of Life.** They're real and they work,
but in a two-minute video they make SOMA look like three apps arguing with each
other, and the viewer files it under "wellness app". That is the wrong shelf and
it is very hard to get off once you're on it.

**Market size slides.** They'll ask if they want the number. Leading with it
signals you have nothing to show, and you do.

**The word "revolutionary", and any sentence beginning "Introducing".**

---

## Recording notes

- **iPhone, TestFlight build.** Do Not Disturb on, battery charged, so the
  status bar is clean. Screen Recording from Control Centre.
- **Record 3–4 minutes of real use**, then cut. Trying to perform it in one take
  is how you end up with a video of someone tapping nervously.
- **Watch the Synergy Scan take.** If Groq's rate limit refuses the call, the app
  silently falls back to five hardcoded conversation turns — you would film a
  template without knowing. Check the dialogue names things specific to those two
  people. If it reads generic, discard the take and do it again.
- **Do the recording when nothing else is hitting the API.** One scan spends more
  output tokens than the per-minute ceiling allows.
